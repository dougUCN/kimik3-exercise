The attention term is separate and it wins at long context. The 208.4 GFLOP above covers every weight matmul. The 24 MLA layers add 1.47M × s FLOP on top, where s is context length — 6.0 GFLOP at 4K, but 1,546 GFLOP at 1M. The crossover, where attention starts costing more than all 104.2B active parameters, sits at 141K tokens. That single number is the argument for the 69:24 hybrid.
